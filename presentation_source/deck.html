What could derail progress

Challenges and mitigation

Identity and interpretation create the largest risks

Identity

A tax lot can span multiple buildings

Mitigation   Add GeoSearch + BIN confirmation

Interpretation

A complaint can be read as a verified condition

Mitigation   Separate labels + comprehension test

Models

Predictions can drift and explanations can overreach

Mitigation   Time split, calibration, and claim review

Coverage and liability

Reporting bias and strong labels can harm trust

Mitigation   Neutral language, no safety score, expert review
